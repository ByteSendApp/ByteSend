## Overview

You can sign in to ByteSend with your email address or with any connected GitHub, Google or Discord account. Manage them in **Settings → Account → Connected Accounts**.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | Links another provider to your ByteSend account, so you can sign in with it |
| **Reconnect** | Signs in with the provider again to refresh the email address and avatar it shares with ByteSend |
| **Disconnect** | Removes the provider. You can still sign in with your email address |
| **Use this email** | Makes the provider's email address your ByteSend email, after verification |

Which providers appear depends on how your ByteSend instance is configured.

---

## Changing your email to a provider's email

If you changed the email address on your GitHub, Google or Discord account:

1. Select **Reconnect** next to the provider so ByteSend sees the new address.
2. Select **Use this email**.
3. Enter the code sent to your current email, then the code sent to the new one.

You need access to both addresses, the same as any other email change. If two-factor authentication is on and you have lost access to your current email, you can use one of your recovery codes in place of the first code.

---

## Verified emails only

ByteSend only accepts a provider sign-in when the provider has verified that email address. If you see *"That account's email address isn't verified with the provider"*, verify the email in your GitHub, Google or Discord settings, then try again, or sign in with your email address instead.

<Note>
This protects your account: without it, anyone could add your email address to a new account on another service and use it to sign in to ByteSend as you.
</Note>
