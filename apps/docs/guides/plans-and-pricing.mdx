ByteSend Cloud has a free plan and three paid plans. Prices are in US dollars, and Checkout shows set prices in pounds or euros if you're in the UK or Europe. The [pricing page](https://bytesend.cloud/#pricing) always has the current prices and lets you estimate your bill.

<Note>
Self-hosted ByteSend has no plans or limits: you pay AWS directly for what you send.
</Note>

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free | $5/mo (£4 / €5) | $20/mo (£16 / €19) | $90/mo (£72 / €85) |
| **Emails included** | 5,000/mo | 25,000/mo | 100,000/mo | 500,000/mo |
| **Daily limit** | 200/day | None | None | None |
| **Extra emails** | Not available | $0.40 per 1,000 transactional, $0.70 per 1,000 marketing | $0.35 / $0.60 per 1,000 | $0.30 / $0.50 per 1,000 |
| **Domains** | 2 | 4 | 12 | 30 |
| **Team members** | 5 | 10 | 30 | 50 |
| **Webhooks** | 3 | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| **Contacts** | 100 | 200 | 1,000 | 10,000 |
| **Contact books** | 5 | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| **Campaigns** | 3 | 30 | 200 | 1,000 |
| **Support** | Community | Community | Priority | Priority |

Every plan includes the REST API, SMTP relay, SDKs, templates, webhooks, analytics and both transactional and marketing email.

Need more domains or seats without changing plan? Extra domains are $1/mo each and extra team members $2/mo each.

## New accounts

To keep ByteSend's shared sending reputation healthy, new **free** teams can send up to **50 emails a day** for their first 7 days. The limit lifts automatically after that, straight away if you upgrade to any paid plan, or sooner if you [contact support](https://discord.gg/C2Q4GHXKQA).

## What happens at your limit

- **Free:** sending stops for the rest of the day or month once you reach the daily or monthly limit, and you're emailed when you get close.
- **Starter, Pro and Scale:** emails beyond your plan's allowance keep sending and are added to your next invoice at the per-1,000 rates above.

Sending also pauses for any plan whose 7-day hard-bounce rate goes above 2% or complaint rate above 0.1%. See [Deliverability](/guides/deliverability).

## Changing plans

Upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from **Settings → Billing** in the [dashboard](https://bytesend.cloud/settings/billing).

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately.
- **Downgrades and cancellations** take effect at the end of the current billing period. If you're over the new plan's limits (for example, more domains than it allows), remove the extras first.

## Billing

Payments are handled by Stripe, and invoices are available under **Settings → Billing**. VAT and sales tax are added at checkout based on your billing address. Businesses can enter a VAT number to have reverse charge applied where it's allowed.

For anything above 500,000 emails a month, or custom limits, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/C2Q4GHXKQA) about a custom plan.

## Legacy plans

Lite and Lifetime are no longer sold. If you already have one, nothing changes: you keep its limits and price for as long as you stay on it. Teams that subscribed before October 2026 also keep the price they signed up at. If you move to a different plan, you can't switch back to a legacy one.
